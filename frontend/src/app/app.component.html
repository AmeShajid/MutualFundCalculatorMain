How This Calculator Works

1

Fetch Beta (β)

We pull the fund's beta from Newton Analytics — this measures how volatile the fund is relative to the S&P 500. A beta of 1.0 means it moves with the market; above 1.0 means more volatile, below means less.

2

Get Market Return (Rm)

We fetch 1-year historical price data from Yahoo Finance and calculate the actual return rate: (last close − first close) / first close.

3

Apply CAPM Formula

The Capital Asset Pricing Model gives us the expected return: r = Rf + β × (Rm − Rf), where Rf is the risk-free rate (4.5%, based on U.S. Treasury yields).

4

Continuous Compounding

Your future value is calculated as: FV = P × e(r × t). This assumes returns compound continuously over your investment horizon.

This is a simplified educational model. Actual investment returns depend on many factors not captured here. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
